# Authored fictional data

All fixture documents, messages, companies and identities in `fixtures/dev/` and `fixtures/heldout/` were authored for this project. CedarBridge, MapleHarbor, BirchBeacon, ElmCircuit, AlderAtlas, WillowWorks, JuniperGate, HazelHarbor, CypressCloud, RowanRelay, AspenAnchor, DocsHub, TeamChat, Alex, Priya and Sam are fictional. The fixtures contain no real enterprise documents, customer data, employer content or personal information.

The original authored fixtures are released under the repository's MIT license. Sentinel strings such as `PROCUREMENT_ONLY_CEDAR` are deliberately invented to detect access leaks in tests. They are not credentials or real secrets.

Development data consists of four scenario families across three identities: 12 labeled cases. The M3 actor-held-out corpus has ten different families across those identities: 30 labeled cases, 50 initial source records and two additional distinct historical/replacement versions. All fixture text, semantic labels, injected instructions, invented sentinels, rubrics and mini corruption controls are original authored data under the repository MIT license. No external benchmark dataset has been copied or evaluated. Held-out labels were authored after independent M2 actor/baseline acceptance and remain outside the actor's registered tools. Same-author authorship is disclosed; this is not a blind dataset. Local offline scoring completed 30 examples across two scripted policies and three repeats (180 primary attempts), with 18 separate cache warm-ups. Repeats do not increase the independent example count; no fixture or label was changed after scoring.
